1. Design Concept:
This blocky sheep headphone stand features a voxel sheep head in full block relief over a woolly neck, smooth rounded core hidden between the front and back faces, generated using a 96 x 58 x 209mm, 44mm neck configuration.
Part of the Voxel Headphone Stands collection. Original blocky voxel art — an independent design, not affiliated with or endorsed by any game.

2. Use Suggestion:
The headband drops into the channel behind the sheep's head and rests on the smooth crest, with the ear cups hanging either side of the neck.

3. Print Recommendations:
Print lying on the back face — every overhang in the silhouette becomes flat geometry that way. Recommended layer height: 0.2mm | Infill: 15-20% | No supports required |
PLA prints fine; PETG or ABS is tougher for the clamp and thumbscrew.
