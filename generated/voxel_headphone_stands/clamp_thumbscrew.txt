1. Design Concept:
This clamp thumbscrew features a knurled knob with a printed 6mm-pitch thread, generated using a 55mm thread, no hardware needed configuration.
Part of the Voxel Headphone Stands collection. Original blocky voxel art — an independent design, not affiliated with or endorsed by any game.

2. Use Suggestion:
Threads into the desk clamp to grip the table. No nuts or bolts required.

3. Print Recommendations:
Print knob-down on the bed, threads pointing up. Recommended layer height: 0.2mm | Infill: 15-20% | No supports required |
PLA prints fine; PETG or ABS is tougher for the clamp and thumbscrew.
